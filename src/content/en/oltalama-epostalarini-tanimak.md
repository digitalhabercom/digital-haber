Phishing emails usually create a sense of urgency: "your account will be closed", "your payment failed".

## Checklist

- Read the sender's address carefully.
- Hover over a link to see its real address.
- Do not open attachments you were not expecting.
- If in doubt, contact the organization through its official website.

If you think a message is fake, do not reply, do not click any links, and use your email service's "report" option.
