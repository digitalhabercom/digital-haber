AI assistants are now used for everything from writing to data analysis. But not every tool fits every need.

## 1. Clarify your purpose

Writing text, coding help, research or image generation may not give the best result with the same tool. Decide what you want to do first.

## 2. Read the privacy policy

Check whether the data you share is used to train the model. This is especially important for company data.

## 3. Evaluate the total cost

Free plan limits, the scope of paid plans and API costs should be considered together.

## 4. Always check accuracy

AI output can be wrong. Important information needs to be verified from reliable sources.

## 5. Look at integrations

An assistant that works with the email, document and project tools you already use saves time.
