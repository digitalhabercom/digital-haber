The 3-2-1 rule is a simple, widely accepted framework for backups.

- **3 copies:** The original data and at least two backups.
- **2 different media:** For example, a local disk and cloud storage.
- **1 off-site location:** Somewhere else, in case of fire, theft or failure.

Remember to restore your backups from time to time to test them. An untested backup is a backup you are not sure works.
