You do not need complicated tools to secure your accounts. In most cases, two habits make enough of a difference.

## A separate password for every account

Using the same password in many places puts all your accounts at risk in a single leak. A password manager makes this easy.

## Turn on two-step verification

Even if your password is stolen, a second verification step makes it harder to get into your account. If possible, prefer an authenticator app or a security key over SMS.

## Check regularly

Review the active sessions and connected apps on your accounts from time to time.
