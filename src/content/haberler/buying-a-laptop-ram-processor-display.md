When choosing a laptop, look for balance instead of focusing on a single feature.

- **RAM:** A sufficient amount for daily use determines multitasking performance.
- **Processor:** It only needs to match how you use the machine; you do not always need the most powerful one.
- **Display:** You stare at it for hours, so resolution and brightness affect comfort.
- **Battery:** If portability matters, look at real-world usage times.
