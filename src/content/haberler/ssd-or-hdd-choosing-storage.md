SSDs offer speed and silence, while HDDs offer a low cost per gigabyte.

## Speed

For the operating system and frequently used apps, an SSD noticeably shortens boot and load times.

## Capacity and price

For archives, backups and large media files, an HDD is still an economical solution.

## A practical approach

For many users, the most balanced choice is an SSD as the system disk and a separate HDD or external drive for the archive.
